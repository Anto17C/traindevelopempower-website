Course Overview

Petroleum Projects Economics and Risk Analysis is a 5-day programme that gives professionals the knowledge and practical skills to evaluate, finance and manage risk in oil and gas projects. It blends economic theory with real-world applications across petroleum investment appraisal, financial modelling and risk analysis, relevant to upstream, downstream and integrated projects.

You will work through petroleum investment appraisal and discounted cash flow, NPV and IRR, fiscal systems, financial modelling, sensitivity and scenario analysis, decision trees and Monte Carlo simulation, led by an expert facilitator.

Course Objectives

By the end of the programme, participants will be able to:

  • Understand the economics of petroleum projects
  • Apply financial tools for project appraisal and decision-making
  • Analyse and manage project risks
  • Evaluate the impact of fiscal systems and market dynamics
  • Make informed investment decisions in a volatile environment
Practical Skills You Will Gain
  • Applying NPV, IRR and other investment criteria
  • Building and using petroleum project financial models
  • Conducting sensitivity and scenario analysis
  • Implementing risk management strategies
  • Evaluating project viability under uncertainty

Course Outline

Twelve Modules, Five Days

  1. Petroleum Industry Economics and Investment Fundamentals
  2. Time Value of Money and Discounted Cash Flow Analysis
  3. Capital Budgeting and Investment Appraisal
  4. NPV, IRR, Payback Period and Profitability Index
  5. Petroleum Fiscal Systems, Taxation and Government Take
  6. Petroleum Project Financial Modelling
  7. Oil and Gas Price Forecasting and Sensitivity Analysis
  8. Risk Identification, Assessment and Quantification
  9. Decision Tree Analysis and Expected Monetary Value
  10. Monte Carlo Simulation and Probabilistic Risk Analysis
  11. Reserves Uncertainty and Production Forecasting
  12. Integrated Petroleum Project Economics Case Studies
