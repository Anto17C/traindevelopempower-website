Public Training Terms

These terms apply to seat requests and bookings for our scheduled public training sessions, such as Petroleum Projects Economics and Risk Analysis.

These Public Training Terms apply to scheduled public training sessions run by Train Develop Empower Co., Ltd. (“Train Develop Empower”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), where delegates from different organisations attend the same session. “Client” means the organisation or person registering delegates and paying the invoice. They do not apply to customised in-house training, which is covered by our In-House Training Terms.

1. Booking and Payment

Submitting a seat request is not a booking. We issue the invoice and collect payment. Payment by the invoice due date reserves your seats. The training fee is subject to applicable tax, and the Client is responsible for bank charges. We will confirm the session in writing.

Registering four paying delegates for the same session earns one complimentary seat, as stated on the course page and registration form.

2. If We Cancel or Postpone

If we cancel a session, we refund the full training fee paid to the original payer.

If we postpone or materially change a session, the payer can choose a full training-fee refund or a transfer to the revised session. Our target is to initiate refunds within 10 business days.

3. If You Cancel

Send written notice to iht@traindevelopempower.com. Before we confirm the session in writing, the full training fee is refundable.

After we confirm the session in writing, the refund is:

Written notice before the session startsRefund of training fee
14 or more calendar days100%
7 to 13 calendar days50%
Fewer than 7 calendar days, or a no-show0%

Days are counted before the session starts, using venue-local dates. Applicable rights are unaffected.

4. Substitutions and Group Bookings

A colleague from the same organisation may take a delegate’s seat at no charge with at least two business days’ written notice.

If cancellations leave fewer than four paying delegates, the complimentary seat no longer applies. We will agree the revised booking with the Client before adjusting the invoice or refund.

5. Travel and Visas

Please wait for our written confirmation before booking non-refundable travel. Delegates are responsible for their own visa and travel arrangements and the associated costs. We do not cover costs arising from visa refusal or travel disruption; the standard client cancellation terms above apply.

6. What’s Included

The training fee includes training materials, a certificate, morning and afternoon coffee breaks, and one buffet lunch per training day.

7. Daily Timetable

We will share the daily timetable and joining instructions once the payment is received.

8. Contact Us

Questions about these terms?

Contact us before requesting seats if you would like any provision clarified.
